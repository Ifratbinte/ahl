About us

Who we are

Apex Holdings Limited (AHL) is a holding company that manages manufacturing and export-oriented enterprises spanning frozen & convenience foods, textiles & apparel, chemicals and agro businesses.

The sponsors set up their first export-oriented manufacturing enterprise in 1980 and have grown into a sizable group of companies. As the number of companies grew, AHL was formed and registered in Bangladesh in 1998 to coordinate and keep control over each individual company.

Environmental friendliness

One of the largest privately owned water treatment plants in the country, researching wastewater recycling. Factories use chemicals that follow ZDHC guidelines, and singeing and sublimation reduce dye wastage and chemical use.

Recyclable materials

Global Recycle Standard (GRS) certified, turning pre-consumer waste into new fibre. Materials include recycled polyester, recycled ocean plastic, bamboo fibre and organic cotton, with an online scrap platform run with Reverse Resources.
